## 1. Milan Atelier / Fashion Designers / Cool Minimalist / Luxury Watches

**Visual Prompt**

Editorial photograph inside a top-floor Milan atelier, soft midday light.
Two designers stand at a steel cutting table, one holding a bolt of grey wool
up to the window while the other adjusts the drape with two fingers, neither
speaking. Poured concrete floor, a rail of muslin toiles, a single Eames
shell chair pushed against raw plaster wall. Palette of dove grey, chalk
white and cool graphite. A slim titanium watch rests face-up on the table
edge, its dial catching the window light in a single clean line. Shot on
50mm at f/2, diffused north light through tall steel-framed windows, shallow
depth of field, fine grain. Candid documentary framing — nobody looking at
the lens. Muted contrast, filmic highlight rolloff, premium magazine
editorial quality.

**Social Caption**

Nobody said a word for ten minutes. The fabric was doing all the talking.

**Visual Prompt**
