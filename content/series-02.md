## 1. Lisbon Rooftop Terrace / Restaurateurs / Warm Terracotta / Natural Wine

**Visual Prompt**

Editorial photograph on a Lisbon rooftop terrace, golden hour. Three
restaurateurs share a weathered wooden table, one pouring while another tells a
story with both hands, the third laughing into her shoulder. Hand-painted azulejo
tiles, sun-bleached linen awning, wrought iron railing, red-tiled rooftops
stepping down toward the Tagus. Palette of terracotta, honeyed ochre and faded
cobalt. A bottle of natural wine, unlabelled and slightly cloudy, catches the low
sun beside three mismatched glasses. Shot on 85mm at f/1.8, direct golden-hour
backlight with bounce from the tabletop, shallow depth of field, fine grain.
Candid documentary framing — nobody looking at the lens. Warm muted contrast,
filmic highlight rolloff, premium magazine editorial quality.

**Social Caption**

Service ended an hour ago. Nobody has moved. That's how you know it was a good one.
