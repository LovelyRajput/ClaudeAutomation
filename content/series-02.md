## 1. Lisbon Rooftop Terrace / Chefs / Warm Terracotta / Artisanal Olive Oil

**Visual Prompt**

Editorial photograph on a whitewashed Lisbon rooftop terrace, golden hour. Three
chefs gather around a long weathered table, one tearing bread and dipping it with
a grin, another mid-story with flour still on a forearm, the third laughing into
her shoulder. Hand-painted azulejo tiles, sun-bleached linen awning, clay pots of
rosemary, the Tagus glinting between rooftops. Palette of terracotta, burnt
saffron and chalk white. A squat glass bottle of cold-pressed olive oil sits at the
table's center, late sun igniting the green-gold liquid from within. Shot on 85mm
at f/1.8, low directional sunlight with soft bounce off the walls, shallow depth
of field, fine 35mm grain. Candid documentary framing — nobody looking at the
lens. Muted contrast, filmic highlight rolloff, premium magazine editorial quality.

**Social Caption**

Nobody checked the time. The bread was warm and the story wasn't finished.

## 4. Reykjavik Design Studio / Industrial Designers / Soft Nordic Blue / Ceramic Tableware

**Social Caption**

Months of drafts, and it came down to how it felt in two hands. Worth every one of
