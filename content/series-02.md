## 1. Marrakech Riad Rooftop / Textile Designers / Sun-Baked Terracotta / Handloom Textiles

**Visual Prompt**

Editorial photograph on a Marrakech riad rooftop, late afternoon with the call
to prayer just fading. Two textile designers kneel either side of a low brass
tray table, unrolling a length of handloom cloth between them, one testing the
weight of the weave in her fingers while the other watches the light move
across it. Tadelakt plaster walls the color of clay, a striped wool rug
underfoot, a single potted olive tree throwing soft shadow. Palette of
terracotta, saffron and whitewashed cream, with the deep blue of the coming
evening at the roofline. The cloth itself is the hero, its hand-dyed stripes
catching the low sun as it unspools. Shot on 50mm at f/1.8, warm raking
daylight with a bounce off the plaster wall, shallow depth of field, fine
grain. Candid documentary framing — nobody looking at the lens. Warm contrast,
filmic highlight rolloff, premium magazine editorial quality.

**Social Caption**

to see.
