## 1. Milan Design Atelier / Furniture Designers / Warm Terracotta / Leather Goods

**Visual Prompt**

Editorial photograph inside a Milan design atelier, golden hour light low
through tall arched windows. Two furniture designers stand over a
half-upholstered chair frame, one running a palm along the raw wood joint
while the other holds a swatch of saddle leather up against it, comparing
grain. Plaster walls the color of raw terracotta, exposed oak beams, sawdust
catching the light on the concrete floor. Palette of terracotta, walnut and
warm ivory. A worn leather satchel rests open on a stool nearby, tools spilling
from it, the hide dark and supple where hands have worn it smooth. Shot on
50mm at f/2, warm directional window light with soft fill from a white
reflector, shallow depth of field, fine 35mm grain. Candid documentary
framing — nobody looking at the lens. Muted contrast, filmic highlight
rolloff, premium magazine editorial quality.

**Social Caption**

You can tell which chair took longest by which one nobody wants to stop
touching.
