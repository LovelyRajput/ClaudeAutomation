## 1. Milan Rooftop Terrace / Furniture Designers / Terracotta Dusk / Espresso

**Visual Prompt**

Editorial photograph on a Milan rooftop terrace, golden hour sliding into dusk.
Two furniture designers sit on opposite ends of a low travertine bench, one
turning a walnut chair-arm prototype in her hands while the other explains a
curve with a slow, sweeping gesture. Sun-warmed terracotta tiles, linen
awning moving in the breeze, hand-plastered ochre wall. Palette of burnt
sienna, saffron and warm bone. Two espressos in thick white porcelain cups
rest on the bench, a thread of steam catching the last low light. Shot on 85mm
at f/1.8, raking sunset light with open-shade fill, shallow depth of field,
fine film grain. Candid documentary framing — nobody looking at the lens.
Soft contrast, filmic highlight rolloff, premium magazine editorial quality.

**Social Caption**

You can't rush a curve. You can only sit with someone who sees it too.
