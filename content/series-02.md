## 1. New York Rooftop Kitchen / Chefs / Golden Amber / Handmade Ceramics

**Visual Prompt**

Editorial photograph on a Manhattan rooftop test kitchen at golden hour. Two
chefs stand at a scarred steel pass, one plating with tweezers mid-motion, the
other tasting straight off a spoon and already smiling before swallowing.
Exposed ductwork, a wall of copper pans, steam rising off a pot just out of
frame. Palette of golden amber, charred oak and soot black, skyline haze
picking up the last of the sun. A stack of handmade ceramic bowls sits at the
pass edge, one glazed rim catching the light. Shot on 50mm at f/1.8, warm
low-angle sun with a cool bounce from the skyline, shallow depth of field,
fine 35mm grain. Candid documentary framing — nobody looking at the lens.
Rich contrast, filmic highlight rolloff, premium magazine editorial quality.

**Social Caption**

Nobody plated for the camera. They plated because it mattered, and someone
happened to be watching.
