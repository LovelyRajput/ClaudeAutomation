## 1. Milan Design Atelier / Furniture Designers / Warm Terracotta / Leather Goods

**Visual Prompt**

Editorial photograph inside a Milan design atelier, golden hour light slanting
through tall arched windows. Two furniture designers crouch beside a
half-upholstered chair frame, one running a hand along the raw walnut arm
while the other stitches a leather sample by hand, both absorbed. Travertine
flooring, raw plaster walls, drafting tools scattered across a zinc-topped
bench. Palette of burnt terracotta, warm walnut and bone white. A cognac
leather portfolio case rests open on the bench, tools laid across its lining,
catching the low sun. Shot on 35mm at f/2, warm directional daylight with
soft bounce off the plaster, shallow depth of field, fine 35mm grain. Candid
documentary framing — nobody looking at the lens. Muted contrast, filmic
highlight rolloff, premium magazine editorial quality.

**Social Caption**

The chair isn't finished until someone who loves it says so out loud.
