## 1. Milan Design Atelier / Fashion Designers / Warm Terracotta / Leather Goods

**Visual Prompt**

Editorial photograph inside a Milan design atelier, golden hour light low
through tall arched windows. Two designers stand at a long cutting table, one
running a thumb along a fresh seam while the other holds a pattern piece up
against the light, both silent, absorbed. Aged terracotta tile, exposed
timber beams, a wall of hide swatches in graduated tan. Palette of burnt
terracotta, saddle brown and warm ivory. A half-finished leather satchel
sits open on the table, tools fanned beside it, the leather's grain catching
a hard sidelight. Shot on 50mm at f/1.8, low raking sun through the
windows, warm practical fill from a desk lamp, shallow depth of field, fine
35mm grain. Candid documentary framing — nobody looking at the lens. Rich
contrast, filmic highlight rolloff, premium magazine editorial quality.

**Social Caption**

The stitch that takes an extra hour is the one nobody notices and everybody
feels. That's the whole craft.
