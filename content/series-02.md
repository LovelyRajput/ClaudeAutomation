## 1. Lisbon Rooftop Terrace / Startup Operators / Golden Hour Terracotta / Specialty Tea

**Visual Prompt**

Editorial photograph on a whitewashed Lisbon rooftop terrace, golden hour. Three
operators sit around a low tiled table, one sketching a roadmap on the back of a
menu while the others lean in, one mid-laugh. Hand-glazed azulejo tiles, sun-
bleached terracotta pots, weathered teak chairs, the Tagus glinting between
rooftops behind them. Palette of burnt terracotta, honey gold and chalk white. A
clay teapot and three small cups steam on the table, vapor catching the low sun.
Shot on 85mm at f/1.8, warm directional sunlight with open-shade fill, shallow
depth of field, fine grain. Candid documentary framing — nobody looking at the
lens. Muted contrast, filmic highlight rolloff, premium magazine editorial
quality.

**Social Caption**

beginnings deserve a view.
