## 1. Lisbon Rooftop Terrace / Architects / Golden Terracotta / Artisanal Wine

**Visual Prompt**

Editorial photograph on a Lisbon rooftop terrace, golden hour. Three architects
gather around a weathered wooden table, one unrolling a site plan and pinning its
corner with a glass, another tracing a line with a fingertip while the third
laughs at the suggestion. Hand-painted azulejo tiles, sun-bleached terracotta
roofs stepping down to the Tagus, a trailing bougainvillea. Palette of burnt
sienna, honey gold and faded cobalt. A bottle of Douro red and three stemless
glasses catch the low sun, the wine glowing ruby at the edges. Shot on 85mm at
f/1.8, natural backlight with warm bounce off the tiled wall, shallow depth of
field, fine 35mm grain. Candid documentary framing — nobody looking at the lens.
Muted contrast, filmic highlight rolloff, premium magazine editorial quality.

**Social Caption**
