## 1. Marrakech Riad / Textile Designers / Terracotta & Indigo / Handwoven Rugs

**Visual Prompt**

Editorial photograph in the courtyard of a Marrakech riad, golden hour. Two
textile designers kneel over a half-finished rug spread across worn zellige
tile, one running fingers along a new knot pattern while the other reaches for
a skein of indigo wool, mid-sentence. Carved cedar archways, a trickling
central fountain, stacks of raw wool baskets against a whitewashed wall.
Palette of burnt terracotta, deep indigo and sun-bleached plaster. The rug
itself is the hero, its pile catching low sun at a raking angle that shows
every knot. Shot on 35mm at f/2, warm directional sunlight through the
courtyard opening, soft shadow falloff, fine 35mm grain. Candid documentary
framing — nobody looking at the lens. Rich contrast, filmic highlight
rolloff, premium magazine editorial quality.

**Social Caption**

Ten thousand knots and neither of us counted a single one. That's what
happens when the work is enough.
