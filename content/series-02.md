## 1. Milan Design Atelier / Industrial Designers / Warm Terracotta / Leather Goods

**Visual Prompt**

Editorial photograph inside a Milan design atelier, golden late afternoon.
Two industrial designers stand at a scarred oak workbench, one turning a
leather sample over in her hands while the other sketches a hinge detail
beside her, both absorbed, neither speaking. Raw plaster walls, a wall of
hand tools on pegboard, brass fittings scattered across the bench. Palette of
terracotta, saddle tan and warm ivory. A half-finished leather satchel sits
clamped in a bench vise, its edges freshly burnished and catching the light.
Shot on 50mm at f/2, low warm sidelight through tall atelier windows, shallow
depth of field, fine grain. Candid documentary framing — nobody looking at
the lens. Warm contrast, filmic highlight rolloff, premium magazine
editorial quality.

**Social Caption**

The stitch you rip out three times is the one nobody will ever notice.
That's exactly the point.
