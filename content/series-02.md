## 1. Reykjavik Glass Studio / Ceramicists / Icy Arctic Blue / Handmade Ceramics

**Visual Prompt**

Editorial photograph inside a Reykjavik glass-walled ceramics studio, midday
in deep winter. Two ceramicists stand at a long steel worktable, one turning
a half-glazed bowl in raking light while the other reaches past to steady it,
both absorbed, neither posing. Raw concrete floor, birch shelving lined with
greenware, a bank of north-facing windows fogged at the edges with cold.
Palette of glacial blue, bone white and the faint ash-grey of wet clay. A
finished ceramic vessel sits drying on the table's corner, its unglazed rim
catching a hard sliver of daylight. Shot on 50mm at f/2, flat arctic window
light, minimal fill, shallow depth of field, fine grain. Candid documentary
framing — nobody looking at the lens. Muted contrast, filmic highlight
rolloff, premium magazine editorial quality.

**Social Caption**

The bowl isn't finished until someone else's hands agree it is. That's the
part no kiln can do.
