## 1. Lisbon Rooftop / Startup Operators / Sun-Bleached Terracotta / Specialty Wine

**Visual Prompt**

Editorial photograph on a Lisbon rooftop terrace, golden hour. Three startup
operators sit around a low tiled table, one laughing mid-story, another
topping up glasses without breaking eye contact with the speaker. Whitewashed
parapet, hand-painted azulejo tiles, weathered teak chairs, red-tiled roofs
falling away toward the Tagus. Palette of terracotta, sun-bleached cream and
Atlantic blue. A bottle of Douro red and three stemless glasses catch the low
sun, the wine glowing garnet at the edges. Shot on 50mm at f/2, direct
low-angle sunlight with soft bounce off the wall, shallow depth of field, fine
film grain. Candid documentary framing — nobody looking at the lens. Warm,
muted contrast, filmic highlight rolloff, premium magazine editorial quality.

**Social Caption**
