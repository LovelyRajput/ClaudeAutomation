## 1. Milan Design Atelier / Industrial Designers / Warm Terracotta / Furniture Prototypes

**Visual Prompt**

Editorial photograph inside a Milan design atelier, golden hour spilling
through tall arched windows. Two industrial designers circle a half-finished
chair prototype on a rolling cart, one crouched to run a hand along a bent
plywood curve, the other sketching a revision on a clipboard braced against a
raised knee. Poured concrete floor, raw steel shelving stacked with foam
maquettes, a length of unbleached canvas draped over a workbench. Palette of
warm terracotta, bleached ash and soot black. The prototype chair itself is
the hero object, its joinery catching a hard rake of late sun. Shot on 35mm at
f/2, direct late-afternoon window light with a bounced fill card, shallow
depth of field, fine 35mm grain. Candid documentary framing — nobody looking
at the lens. Rich warm contrast, filmic highlight rolloff, premium magazine
editorial quality.

**Social Caption**

The chair wasn't right until someone knelt down to check how it felt from
underneath. That's the part nobody sees.
