## 1. Lisbon Rooftop / Film Directors / Sun-Washed Terracotta / Vintage Cameras

**Visual Prompt**

Editorial photograph on a Lisbon rooftop terrace, golden hour in early autumn.
Two directors sit on a low tiled wall, one sketching a shot list on the back of
a call sheet while the other, laughing, corrects it from memory. Hand-painted
azulejo tiles, sun-bleached stucco, a lemon tree in a cracked clay pot, the
Tagus glinting between rooftops. Palette of terracotta, faded cobalt and warm
honey. A vintage 35mm rangefinder rests on the wall between them, its chrome
catching the last low sun. Shot on 50mm at f/2, direct golden-hour light with
open-shade fill, shallow depth of field, fine 35mm grain. Candid documentary
framing — nobody looking at the lens. Soft contrast, filmic highlight rolloff,
premium magazine editorial quality.

**Social Caption**
