## 1. Milan Design Atelier / Furniture Designers / Warm Terracotta / Hand-thrown Ceramics

**Visual Prompt**

Editorial photograph inside a Milan design atelier, early afternoon light
cutting low through tall shutters. Two furniture designers crouch beside a
half-finished walnut chair frame, one running a hand along the joint while the
other sketches an adjustment on butcher paper taped to the floor. Raw plaster
walls, sawdust caught in a shaft of light, waxed canvas drop cloths folded over
a stool. Palette of terracotta, warm walnut and chalk white. A hand-thrown
ceramic bowl sits on the workbench, glaze pooled unevenly at its base, holding
a scatter of brass fittings. Shot on 50mm at f/2, hard directional daylight
through shutter slats, deep shadow falloff, fine 35mm grain. Candid
documentary framing — nobody looking at the lens. Rich contrast, filmic
highlight rolloff, premium magazine editorial quality.

**Social Caption**

The chair isn't finished. Neither is the conversation about it. Best days
are the ones spent inside both.
