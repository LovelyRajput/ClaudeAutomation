## 1. Milan Atelier / Fashion Designers / Warm Terracotta / Hand-Sketched Patterns

**Visual Prompt**

Editorial photograph inside a top-floor Milan atelier, golden hour light
angling low through tall shuttered windows. Two designers stand at a cutting
table strewn with muslin and pinned toiles, one holding a length of fabric
against the light while the other marks a seam line freehand, chalk dust
still settling. Terracotta-tiled floor, whitewashed brick, a rolling rack of
half-finished garments soft-focused behind them. Palette of burnt terracotta,
raw linen and dusty rose. A hand-sketched pattern sheet lies open on the
table, pencil lines and pinned swatches catching the warm light. Shot on
50mm at f/1.8, low raking window light with a soft bounce fill, shallow
depth of field, fine 35mm grain. Candid documentary framing — nobody looking
at the lens. Muted contrast, filmic highlight rolloff, premium magazine
editorial quality.

**Social Caption**

The pattern only works once someone else can see what you meant. That's the
part worth showing up for.
