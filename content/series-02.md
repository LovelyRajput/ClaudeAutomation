## 1. New York Rooftop / Venture Capitalists / Amber Dusk / Leather Portfolios

**Visual Prompt**

Editorial photograph on a Manhattan rooftop terrace at golden hour. Two
venture capitalists stand at the parapet rail, one flipping through a leather
portfolio while the other studies the skyline, mid-laugh at something
unscripted. Weathered cedar decking, a low steel firepit unlit, potted olive
trees catching the last light. Palette of amber, charcoal and warm gold, the
skyline behind them blurring into a haze of dusk. A worn leather portfolio
lies open on the rail's ledge, pages fluttering slightly, a fountain pen
resting in the crease. Shot on 85mm at f/1.8, low warm sidelight from the
setting sun, shallow depth of field, fine 35mm grain. Candid documentary
framing — nobody looking at the lens. Rich contrast, filmic highlight
rolloff, premium magazine editorial quality.

**Social Caption**
