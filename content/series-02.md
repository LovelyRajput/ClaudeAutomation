## 1. Milan Design Atelier / Furniture Designers / Warm Terracotta / Leather Goods

**Visual Prompt**

Editorial photograph inside a Milan design atelier, golden hour light low
through tall arched windows. Two furniture designers stand either side of a
half-upholstered chair frame, one running a palm along the exposed frame
while the other holds a swatch book open, mid-debate over the grain. Raw
plaster walls, blackened iron shelving stacked with fabric bolts, a scattering
of walnut offcuts on the concrete floor. Palette of burnt terracotta, warm
sienna and bone white. A finished leather satchel rests on a stool in the
foreground, the tan hide catching a hard edge of sun, stitching visible in
close relief. Shot on 50mm at f/1.8, direct low sun through old glass with a
soft bounce fill, shallow depth of field, fine 35mm grain. Candid documentary
framing — nobody looking at the lens. Warm contrast, filmic highlight
rolloff, premium magazine editorial quality.

**Social Caption**

The chair isn't finished. The conversation about it already is — and that's
the part that lasts.
