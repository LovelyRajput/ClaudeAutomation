## 1. Milan Design Loft / Industrial Designers / Burnished Gold / Leather Goods

**Visual Prompt**

Editorial photograph inside a converted Milan design loft, golden hour. Two
industrial designers stand at a steel workbench, one turning a leather sample
over in her hands while the other points at a sketch pinned above, mid-argument
that's clearly about to resolve into agreement. Raw concrete ceiling, walnut
shelving lined with maquettes, a single Nelson-style pendant throwing warm
light low over the bench. Palette of burnished gold, saddle tan and charcoal.
A hand-stitched leather satchel sits open on the bench, tools scattered beside
it, the brass hardware catching the pendant's glow. Shot on 50mm at f/1.8,
late sun through tall industrial windows with a warm practical fill, shallow
depth of field, fine 35mm grain. Candid documentary framing — nobody looking
at the lens. Rich contrast, filmic highlight rolloff, premium magazine
editorial quality.

**Social Caption**

She turned it over twice before she said it was ready. That's how you know it
actually is.

## 2. Reykjavik Glass Studio / Glassblowers / Deep Indigo / Hand-Blown Lighting

**Visual Prompt**

Editorial photograph inside a Reykjavik glass studio, blue winter dusk pressing

turning the blowpipe steady while the other shapes the molten form with wet
newspaper, both lit from below by the furnace's orange glow against the
indigo dark beyond the glass. Soot-blackened brick, worn steel tools hung in
rows, a rack of cooling pendant shades glowing faintly amber. Palette of deep
indigo, furnace orange and soot black. A finished hand-blown pendant lamp
hangs in the foreground, its bulb warming the glass from within. Shot on 35mm
at f/2, mixed furnace and tungsten light, deep shadow falloff, visible grain
in the dark. Candid documentary framing — nobody looking at the lens. Rich
contrast, filmic highlight rolloff, premium magazine editorial quality.

**Social Caption**

Four hands, one breath of air, and something that didn't exist an hour ago.
That's the whole craft.

## 4. Kyoto Tea House / Ceramicists / Monochrome Noir / Stoneware Teaware

**Visual Prompt**

contrast, filmic highlight rolloff, premium magazine editorial quality.

**Social Caption**

Nobody said a word for twenty minutes. The bowl still came out right. Some
things are taught with hands, not sentences.
