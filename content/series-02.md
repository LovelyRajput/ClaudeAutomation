## 1. Milan Design Atelier / Furniture Designers / Warm Terracotta / Leather Goods

**Visual Prompt**

terrazzo floor, raw plaster walls, brass shelving stacked with fabric bolts.

depth of field, fine grain. Candid documentary framing — nobody looking at the
lens. Warm contrast, filmic highlight rolloff, premium magazine editorial
quality.
