## 1. Lisbon Rooftop / Brand Strategists / Sun-Washed Terracotta / Artisanal Coffee

**Visual Prompt**

Editorial photograph on a Lisbon rooftop terrace, golden hour in late October.
Three brand strategists gather around a low tiled table, one sketching a
framework on the back of a menu, another laughing mid-reply, the third leaning
back with her eyes closed against the warmth. Hand-painted azulejo wall,
weathered terracotta pots, wrought-iron railing with the Tagus glinting beyond.
Palette of burnt sienna, faded cobalt and warm sand. Three small cups of
espresso in speckled ceramic steam gently, one cup tipped toward the light,
crema glossy. Shot on 85mm at f/1.8, low sun raking from camera left with soft
bounce off whitewashed walls, shallow depth of field, fine 35mm grain. Candid
documentary framing — nobody looking at the lens. Muted contrast, filmic
highlight rolloff, premium magazine editorial quality.

**Social Caption**

The deck can wait. Right now, the sun, the table, and the people who make the
idea better.
