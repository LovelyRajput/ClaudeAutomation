## 1. Milan Atelier / Fashion Designers / Cool Minimalist / Luxury Watches

**Visual Prompt**

Editorial photograph inside a top-floor Milan atelier, soft overcast daylight.
Two designers stand at a steel cutting table, one adjusting the drape of a
half-pinned toile while the other studies it with arms crossed, head tilted.
Polished concrete floor, a rolling rack of muslin samples, floor-to-ceiling
windows with the Duomo's spire just visible in haze. Palette of bone white,
graphite and cool steel blue. A slim titanium watch catches the flat daylight
on an outstretched wrist as a pin is set. Shot on 50mm at f/2, diffuse north
light, shallow depth of field, fine grain. Candid documentary framing —
nobody looking at the lens. Muted contrast, filmic highlight rolloff, premium
magazine editorial quality.

**Social Caption**

The pin goes in, the whole silhouette clicks into place. Small corrections,
made together, are still the job.

## 2. Singapore Rooftop Garden / Tech Founders / Vibrant Neon / Tablets

**Visual Prompt**

Editorial photograph on a rooftop garden terrace above Singapore's financial
district, just after sunset. Three founders sit on low rattan stools around a
teak bench, one turning a tablet toward the others mid-explanation, the rest
leaning in. Tropical foliage silhouetted against a skyline lit in magenta and
cyan signage, humid air softening every edge. Palette of deep jungle green cut
by hot pink and electric blue neon bleed. The tablet's screen throws a cool
glow across three faces. Shot on 35mm at f/1.8, mixed neon and ambient dusk
light, deep bokeh into the skyline, visible grain in shadow. Candid
documentary framing — nobody looking at the lens. Rich contrast, filmic
highlight rolloff, premium magazine editorial quality.

**Social Caption**

Nobody planned to still be up here at nine. The idea just wasn't done with
them yet.

**Social Caption**

## 4. New York Loft / Product Designers / Deep Emerald / Notebooks

**Visual Prompt**

Editorial photograph inside a converted SoHo loft studio, golden hour light
raking low through cast-iron windows. Two product designers stand at a pinup

the other reaches to move a page half a foot to the left. Original tin
ceiling, worn oak floorboards, a deep emerald velvet reading chair in the
corner catching the last light. Palette of emerald, aged brass and warm
shadow. An open notebook rests on a nearby stool, corner curling, pen resting
in the crease. Shot on 35mm at f/1.8, low golden-hour sidelight, long soft
shadows, fine grain. Candid documentary framing — nobody looking at the lens.
Rich contrast, filmic highlight rolloff, premium magazine editorial quality.

**Social Caption**

Moved one sketch half a foot and the whole wall finally agreed with itself.
That's the moment worth keeping.
