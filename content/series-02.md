## 1. Milan Design Atelier / Furniture Designers / Warm Terracotta / Leather Goods

**Visual Prompt**

Editorial photograph inside a Milan design atelier, late afternoon light angling
low through arched factory windows. Two furniture designers crouch beside an
unfinished walnut chair frame, one running a palm along the joinery while the
other sketches a revision on butcher paper taped to the floor. Raw plaster
walls, oiled walnut shavings underfoot, a wall of hung leather hides in graded
tan. Palette of terracotta, saddle tan and warm plaster white. A finished
leather satchel rests open on a stool, tools spilling half out, the leather
catching a hard rim of afternoon light. Shot on 50mm at f/2, warm directional
daylight with bounced fill off the plaster, shallow depth of field, fine 35mm
grain. Candid documentary framing — nobody looking at the lens. Muted contrast,
filmic highlight rolloff, premium magazine editorial quality.

**Social Caption**

The chair isn't finished. The conversation about it already is. That's the part
that was worth showing up for.
