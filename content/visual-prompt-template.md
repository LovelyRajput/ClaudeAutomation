# Editorial Scene Template — Visual Prompt Engineering

Reusable structure for generating high-end editorial social posts. Swap the four
key variables; keep the sophisticated, cinematic tone constant.

## Key Variables

| Variable | Purpose | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Setting** | Location, architecture, time of day | Tokyo High-Rise, London Creative Studio, Paris Cafe |
| **Industry / Professionals** | Who is in frame and how they carry themselves | Tech Founders, Architects, Fashion Designers |
| **Mood / Palette** | Color grade and emotional register | Cool Minimalist, Deep Emerald, Vibrant Neon |
| **Product / Focus** | The hero object the composition orbits | Artisanal Coffee, Tablets / Tech, Luxury Watches |

## Reference Scene — "Belgian Bistro"

> **Visual Prompt:** Editorial photograph inside a warm Belgian bistro on a
> late winter afternoon. Two colleagues lean across a small marble bistro table,
> mid-conversation, one gesturing with an easy laugh. Aged brass fixtures,
> oxblood leather banquettes, fogged windowpanes with Brussels streetlight
> bleeding through. Palette of warm amber, deep burgundy and aged cream. Two
> Belgian ales rest between them, condensation catching the light, a shared
> plate of frites half-finished. Shot on 85mm at f/1.8, natural window light
> with a soft practical fill, shallow depth of field, fine 35mm grain. Candid
> documentary framing — nobody looking at the lens. Muted contrast, filmic
> highlight rolloff, premium magazine editorial quality.
>
> **Social Caption:** The best conversations never make it onto the agenda.
> Here's to the afternoons that end up mattering most.

## Prompt Skeleton

Assemble in this order — each clause does distinct work:

1. **Format + place** — "Editorial photograph inside [Setting], [time of day]."
2. **Human moment** — two or three [Professionals], mid-action, candid, never posed.
3. **Material detail** — three concrete textures native to the setting.
4. **Palette** — name three colors drawn from [Mood / Palette].
5. **Hero object** — [Product / Focus], placed naturally in the scene, lit deliberately.
6. **Camera** — lens, aperture, light source, depth of field, grain.
7. **Quality tags** — candid documentary framing, filmic rolloff, premium editorial.

## Caption Rules

- One or two lines. No hashtag walls, no emoji.
- Lead with a human truth, close with the aspirational turn.
- Reference *connection* and *the rewarding moment*, never the product's features.
- Sound like a person worth listening to, not a brand worth muting.
