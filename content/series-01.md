## 1. Tokyo High-Rise / Tech Founders / Cool Minimalist / Tablets

**Visual Prompt**

Editorial photograph on the 41st floor of a Tokyo high-rise, blue hour. Three
founders stand around a low concrete table, one mid-sentence with a hand open in
explanation, another leaning in with arms folded, listening hard. Floor-to-ceiling
glass, Shinjuku's grid dissolving into haze behind them, brushed steel column,
pale oak flooring, a single ceramic vessel with one branch. Palette of cool slate,
soft white and glacial blue, broken only by the amber pinpricks of the city below.
A slim tablet rests screen-up on the concrete, its glow lifting the underside of a
sleeve. Shot on 50mm at f/2, ambient window light with a low practical fill,
shallow depth of field, fine grain. Candid documentary framing — nobody looking at
the lens. Muted contrast, filmic highlight rolloff, premium magazine editorial
quality.

**Social Caption**

The building emptied out an hour ago. The idea didn't.

## 2. London Creative Studio / Architects / Deep Emerald / Artisanal Coffee

**Visual Prompt**

Editorial photograph inside a converted London warehouse studio, mid-morning.
Two architects bend over a drafting table strewn with trace paper and a
half-built basswood model, one drawing a correction freehand while the other
watches, already nodding. Exposed brick, blackened steel window frames, deep
emerald velvet on a worn studio sofa in the background. Palette of emerald,
oxidized brass and paper cream. Two flat whites sit at the table edge in
handthrown stoneware, one with a fingerprint of crema on the rim. Shot on 35mm at
f/2.2, north-facing daylight through industrial glazing, soft falloff into the
room's depth, fine 35mm grain. Candid documentary framing — nobody looking at the
lens. Muted contrast, filmic highlight rolloff, premium magazine editorial quality.

**Social Caption**

Someone sketches over your drawing and makes it better. That's not a critique —
that's the whole job.

## 3. Paris Cafe / Fashion Designers / Vibrant Neon / Luxury Watches

**Visual Prompt**

Editorial photograph at a corner cafe in the 3rd arrondissement, just after dark.
Three designers crowd one marble table on the terrace, fabric swatches fanned
between the glasses, one holding a sample up to catch the light from the neon
sign overhead. Rattan chairs, brass rail, rain still beading on the awning.
Palette of hot magenta and electric cyan from the signage, grounded in wet black
pavement and warm interior tungsten. A steel dress watch catches the neon on an
outstretched wrist, the crystal throwing a hard pink highlight. Shot on 85mm at
f/1.4, mixed neon and tungsten sources, deep bokeh of the street beyond, visible
grain in the shadows. Candid documentary framing — nobody looking at the lens.
Rich contrast, filmic highlight rolloff, premium magazine editorial quality.

**Social Caption**

Held the swatch up to the light and everyone went quiet at the same second.
Some nights the work finds you.

## 4. Copenhagen Harbour Office / Product Designers / Warm Neutral / Notebooks

**Visual Prompt**

Editorial photograph in a Copenhagen harbour-front office, late afternoon in
autumn. Two product designers sit side by side on a windowsill bench, a shared
notebook open across both their knees, one tracing a flow with a pen while the
other laughs at something just said. Pale ash paneling, a wool throw, water
reflections moving across the ceiling. Palette of oat, sand and soft terracotta,
with the grey-green of the harbour outside. The notebook's open spread is the
brightest surface in frame, ink still wet. Shot on 50mm at f/1.8, low raking
daylight off the water, gentle shadow gradient, fine grain. Candid documentary
framing — nobody looking at the lens. Muted contrast, filmic highlight rolloff,
premium magazine editorial quality.

**Social Caption**

Two people, one notebook, and the version of the plan that finally works.
